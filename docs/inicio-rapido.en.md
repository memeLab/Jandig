# Quick Start

1.  Go to [https://jandig.app](https://jandig.app) and create an account.

2.  Click "Create Jandig Artwork". You don't need to upload your own
    Marker or Object yet — pick an existing Marker and Object from the
    platform to quickly try out how it works. The Jandig Artwork links
    the Marker to the Object for presentation in augmented reality.

3.  In your list of Artworks, open the action menu (three-dot icon) for
    the Artwork you just created and click "Preview". This opens the
    camera and shows how the Artwork will look, without needing to
    create an Exhibition.

Point the camera at the chosen Marker and watch the magic happen!

When you're ready to publish for real, upload your own Marker and
Object and create an Exhibition to share your Artworks with the public
— see the full step-by-step in [How to publish content in
Jandig](publicando-obras.en.md).
